Welcome to open-concrete!

Hey there! Just a friendly heads-up: this is strictly an educational tool 🏗️. It's designed to help students and curious minds explore the math behind reinforced concrete design.

Full Disclosure: This is very much a work in progress. Bugs, glitches, and weird edge cases are definitely lurking in the shadows! 🐛 Seriously, you'd be much better off cracking open a real textbook 📚 than relying on this for actual professional design. If you use this without knowing exactly what you're doing... well, may God bless your soul! 🙏✨

Always consult a licensed Professional Engineer (PE) for actual construction. 📐 - Daniel
